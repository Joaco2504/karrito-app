## Brand & Style

This design system defines an energetic, high-contrast, modern grocery retail experience tailored for speed and visual vibrancy. Rejecting the clinical greens, sterile whites, and conservative blues typical of supermarket apps, it adopts an assertive identity built around Deep Violet Berry and Neon Papaya.

The personality balances quick-turn utility with playful culinary delight:
- **Audience:** Contemporary urban shoppers, busy households, and culinary enthusiasts navigating fast-paced physical aisles or building weekly digital carts.
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, tactile, electric, and immediate. The interface mimics the vibrant dynamism of boutique mercaditos combined with the razor-sharp clarity of next-generation digital commerce.
- **Lighting and Legibility:** Tuned specifically for high-glare environments—such as direct sunlight and fluorescent supermarket lighting—using saturated anchor points, deep indigo-slate typography, and high-luminance accent tags.
- **Design Philosophy:** Tactile Pop. Grounded in Material 3 structure, elevated with deep plum containers, warm oat milk backdrops, and unapologetic pill-shaped interactive anchors.

## Colors

The palette relies on intentional chromatic friction: regal, saturated violet anchored against radiant neon papaya and punchy acid-lime accents.

### Role Assignments & Application
- **Primary (`#531CB3` - Deep Violet Berry):** Dominates high-priority structures, primary checkout funnels, navigation bars, and active badge states. It signals stability, quality, and momentum.
- **Secondary (`#FF6238` - Neon Papaya):** Serves as the key conversion trigger. Applied to cart additions, urgent promotions, lightning deals, and interactive step markers. Never diluted into pastel tints for actions.
- **Tertiary (`#9AE600` - Electric Lime):** Strictly an accent for fresh arrivals, organic certifications, nutritional callouts, and savings indicators. Adds crisp freshness without relying on generic supermarket foliage tones.
- **Neutral Core (`#13101E` - Cassis Slate):** Replaces pure black to preserve warmth. Text rendering maintains crisp legibility on top of both pure white cards and tinted purple-gray surfaces.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** `#F9F8F6` (Porcelain Oat Milk) establishes a soft, organic ground that eliminates glare.
- **Elevated Canvas:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure Bright White) isolates individual product cards and modal dialogs.
- **Subtle Surface Container:** `#F2EFFB` (Tinted Lavender Oat) provides soft differentiation for search rails, filter trays, and category groupings without introducing harsh lines.

## Typography

The single-family deployment of **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a consistent, rounded geometric cadence across every viewport. Its wide aperture, generous x-height, and sculpted terminals sustain high readability when scanned quickly in hand-held, one-handed mobile interactions.

### Typographic Rules
- **Numerical Prominence:** Monetary figures and weights use `headline-md` or `label-lg` with `fontWeight: 700`. Decimals and currency symbols remain baseline-aligned for rapid visual parsing.
- **Letter Spacing:** Tighter negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) is reserved exclusively for bold headline tiers to produce a punchy, editorial feel. Labels and tiny badges use positive tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) to prevent letters from clumping.
- **Hierarchy Enforcers:** Product brand names use `label-md` in uppercase with 55% slate opacity, while actual item descriptors use `title-md` in high-contrast solid slate (`#13101E`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a fluid grid designed for rapid thumb interaction and visual scanning:
- **Mobile Handheld (360px – 599px):** 4-column fluid layout with an edge margin of `1rem` and internal gutters of `0.75rem`. Product grids render in a 2-column card stack with dense, thumb-friendly tap targets (minimum 48px hit area).
- **Tablet / Large Mobile (600px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Product cards conform to a 3- or 4-column rhythm.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column constrained fluid grid capped at `1280px` max container width, centering the shopping canvas with `1.5rem` gutters.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Component Breathing Room:** Internal card padding is locked to `space-md` (16px), while tight badge groupings use `space-xs` (4px).
- **Sticky Actions:** Persistent bottom-docked cart bars maintain a minimum clearance of `space-xl` from the bottom scroll threshold to prevent accidental OS gesture dismissal.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages tonal layer stepping paired with warm, chromatic drop shadows rather than neutral grayscale blurs.

### Surface Tiers
1. **Ground Zero (`#F9F8F6`):** The oat canvas hosts inactive background areas and category backsplashes.
2. **Container Tier 1 (`#F2EFFB`):** Low-contrast violet-tinted containers used for grouping elements like order histories, discount clusters, and input backgrounds.
3. **Card Tier 2 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-priority interactives and product containers. Offset with a warm ambient shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(83, 28, 179, 0.06)`.
4. **Floating Overlays & Drawers:** Sticky cart summary bars, flying action buttons, and modal checkout sheets sit on pure white containers with pronounced directional shadows: `0px 12px 32px rgba(19, 16, 30, 0.12)`.

### Border Treatment
Harsh divider lines are replaced by structural surface color shifts. When a container boundary requires explicit demarcation (e.g., active input fields or selected card states), use a 1.5px solid stroke in `#531CB3` or a 1px soft rim in `rgba(83, 28, 179, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces high-curvature, friendly geometries using level `3` (Pill-shaped). Sharp corners are eliminated to evoke an approachable, contemporary look.

- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Absolute pill radius (`9999px` / `rounded-full`). All action targets, stepper quantity selectors, and filter chips utilize fully rounded ends.
- **Product & Merchant Cards:** Defined with `rounded-lg` (2rem / 32px) on outer perimeter boundaries to establish soft, friendly content containers.
- **Inner Asset Frames:** Images inside product cards adapt an inner curvature of `1rem` (16px) to avoid corner clipping while maintaining harmony with the outer card radius.
- **Bottom Sheets & Floating Modals:** Upper corners use `rounded-xl` (3rem / 48px) to reinforce a natural, physical drape as sheets drag up from the canvas base.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill (`9999px`), Neon Papaya (`#FF6238`) background with crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`, `label-lg`). Under active tap, translates down 1px with an escalated inner shadow.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Full pill, Deep Violet Berry (`#531CB3`) with white typography.
- **Tonal Button:** Full pill, Tinted Lavender (`#F2EFFB`) background with Deep Violet text (`#531CB3`). Used for ancillary cart steps and secondary filters.

### Chips & Badges
- **Tag Pills:** Height of 28px, rounded full, horizontal padding `space-sm`.
- **Promo Badge:** Electric Lime (`#9AE600`) background with Cassis Slate (`#13101E`, `label-md`) text to flag deals and member discounts.
- **Filter Chips:** Inactive state uses pure white with a `1px` border in `rgba(83, 28, 179, 0.12)`. Active state fills with Deep Violet Berry (`#531CB3`) and text flips to white.

### Stepper & Quantity Selector
- **Structure:** Pill-shaped capsule containing horizontal minus, quantity, and plus triggers.
- **Compact Form:** 36px total height, Tinted Lavender base (`#F2EFFB`). Plus button uses a solid Neon Papaya circle (`#FF6238`) to direct tap focus toward incremental additions.

### Cards
- **Product Grid Item:** Pure White background (`#FFFFFF`), `rounded-lg` (32px), `space-md` internal padding. Features a clear top-aligned thumbnail frame, title in `title-md`, subtitle in `label-sm` (60% slate), followed by baseline-aligned pricing and an anchored bottom-right "+" pill.
- **Featured Promo Banner:** Deep Violet Berry base (`#531CB3`), pure white heading typography, and an embedded Neon Papaya pill button for instant claim flows.

### Input Fields
- **Search & Text Input:** Height of 52px, full pill shape. Filled with `#F2EFFB` in idle state; transitions to `#FFFFFF` with a 2px outer ring of `#531CB3` on active focus. Search icons sit within a 16px inset in muted Cassis Slate.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkbox:** `rounded-sm` (6px) box. Idle has a 2px outline in `#531CB3` with no fill. Checked state fills with `#531CB3` displaying a crisp white check icon.
- **Radio:** Full circle with a 2px `#531CB3` rim and a centered, solid `#531CB3` dot on active selection.
